# Presentation story — 5-minute and 15-minute versions

## The problem

“Who is predicted to score the most?” is not the same as “what should I actually do with **my** team?” A transfer may look attractive but cost a hit, break position/club constraints, consume flexibility or disregard risks in the next six fixtures.

## Five-minute talk track

1. **The decision, not merely the forecast (45 sec).** The manager owns a specific 15-player squad, has a bank and limited free transfers, and cares about future options as well as immediate points.
2. **Authority and enrichment (45 sec).** Official FPL provides authoritative team and scoring inputs. LiveFPL adds rank/EO context and different freshness clocks. Human eye-test and hunch evidence is recorded with attribution.
3. **What the app contains (45 sec).** The Control Center includes Home, Team, Live, Leagues, Decision Studio, Research and Advanced. It is the Windows-local product, distinct from the ChatGPT documentation plugin.
4. **The decision gate (75 sec).** An AI model proposes route(s), but code checks real owned transfers, budget, unique 15, three-per-club, legal XI, bench and captain/vice. A failure should be repaired once or blocked.
5. **The research idea (45 sec).** Trace pre-model belief → recommendation → manager response → official action → review. Evidence capture exists; complete unbiased causal evaluation does not.
6. **Evidence and limits (45 sec).** Curated V4.1.2 source exists. A point-in-time runtime audit had 42 passes, six warnings and sixteen not tested; a separate classic-league audit had 21/21 configured leagues. Last explicit current-team auth attempt failed; full certification remains open.

## Fifteen-minute extension

Show a conceptual sources-to-rules diagram, illustrate HOLD vs transfer versus paid hit with a **synthetic** team, then discuss why uncertainty, opportunity cost, rank/EO and competitor behavior should not be conflated. End with the research protocol and seek critique.

## What not to claim

Not automated transfers; not guaranteed superior rank; not a production SaaS; not a trained or continuously self-improving forecasting model; not all league types covered; not verified 24/7 monitoring.

**Closing line:** “I built an explainable decision-support workflow that recognizes the difference between a plausible football prediction and a legal, context-aware action.”
