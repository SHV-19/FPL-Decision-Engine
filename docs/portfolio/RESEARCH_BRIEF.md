# Research brief — human × AI decision quality under constraints

**Research framing, not a published empirical finding.**

## Primary question

In a repeated, noisy decision environment with known rules and decision costs, does explainable AI support change human confidence, action choices, calibration and ex-ante decision consistency?

## Mechanism and units

The system can record a manager's prior preferred action and confidence, subsequent model advice, whether the human agreed or changed their mind, attributed evidence, eventual Official action and gameweek review receipts. Units should be **decision opportunities**, defined before seeing outcomes, not cherry-picked successful transfers.

## Feasible hypotheses

1. Hard-rule checking lowers the rate of structurally illegal recommendations.
2. Provenance/context changes confidence calibration or willingness to defer to AI.
3. Human over-reliance, loss aversion, recent-return salience and social-proof pressure can be **investigated** as patterns in stated reasoning; labels are observations, not diagnoses.
4. A model may improve **ex-ante process quality** without improving realized points over a short noisy window.

## What is and is not supported

The historical post-GW5 checkpoint held approximately 709 research events and five close receipts, but only two rows in a legacy decision ledger. This is instrumentation evidence, not a completed normalized study. Outcome/action linkage, denominator definition, exposure/treatment timing, missingness and counterfactual baselines still require construction and review.

## Suggested honest study protocol

- Freeze the relevant source-build, data definitions and time cutoff **before** analysis.
- Define a synthetic/public, consented or otherwise legitimately shareable cohort.
- Specify eligibility and gameweek boundaries; exclude post-hoc information leakage.
- Use a legality baseline and a blinded ex-ante scoring rubric, not realized points alone.
- Record pre-AI choice, model advice, human response, official behavior and follow-up.
- Report missingness, sample size, uncertainty, repeat-measure dependence and confounders.
- Publish methods/code where permitted, not authentic private manager/account data.

## Scope and collaboration

This is a plausible *personal research instrument* rather than proof of causal AI benefit or a validated self-improving forecast. The strongest external ask is critique of experimental design and metrics, not a claim that the research has already established a statistically significant effect.
